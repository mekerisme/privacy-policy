# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how we ("we," "us," or "our") handle user data in connection with our Android application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe user experience.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Non-Personal / Device Data
* **Camera Access (If applicable):** Used solely on-device to scan QR codes/barcodes. Images/video frames are processed locally and never stored, collected, or transmitted to any external servers.
* **Location Data (If applicable):** Used solely to query nearby stations or relevant local transport data. Location data is processed strictly on-device or sent directly to public APIs (e.g., transport authority APIs) without storing user identity.
* **Network Access:** Used to fetch real-time public data (e.g., train schedules) and serve app operations.

### Personal Data
* We **do not** collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App may integrate third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements or collect diagnostic data:
* **Google Play Services**
* **Google AdMob** (for ad delivery)
* **Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics** (for crash reports and performance monitoring)

These third-party providers may automatically collect non-personally identifiable identifiers (such as Advertising ID and IP address) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not store personal data on any server, there is no personal data to retain or delete upon request. Any cached app data stored locally on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app or using your device settings.
